# Retail Shelf Annotation Guidelines

## General Rules

- Annotate all clearly visible products
- Maintain tight bounding boxes around products
- Avoid overlapping annotations across separate products
- Ensure consistent labeling across similar items

## Product Annotation

- Label each product individually
- Use accurate product boundaries
- Annotate partially visible products only if identifiable

## Product Group Annotation

- Group products belonging to the same SKU or brand together
- Maintain accurate grouping boundaries
- Avoid mixing different product categories

## Price Tag Annotation

- Annotate visible price tags clearly
- Avoid including shelf edges or surrounding objects
- Ensure readability-focused annotation

## Shelf Structure Annotation

- Annotate shelf rows consistently
- Mark signage separately from products
- Maintain clean segmentation boundaries

## Quality Standards

- No missing products
- No duplicate annotations
- Correct class assignment
- Accurate boundary alignment
